# Live effectiveness evaluation after the campaign detector

## What does the implementation establish, and what breaks campaign attribution?

### Takeaway

At `2b426d3d`, TRACE has an experimental persistent campaign rule and chronological student tooling, but `just study` explicitly disables that rule. A live effectiveness study needs campaign-specific orchestration and repaired attribution before real teacher collection and GPU student experiments can support a capability or economic-cost claim. The inspected working tree had no changes relative to that revision before this note. Sources below are local implementation citations, inspected on 2026-11-05. ## What study would establish a limited capability or economic penalty?

### Takeaway

The scientific question is whether campaign throttling changes the resources needed to reach a preregistered useful student capability, at measured comparable legitimate utility. Detection scores, fewer successful requests and mock teacher suppression establish progressively stronger software/access facts, but none alone establishes an economic penalty. [TRACE comparison protocol](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/docs/evaluation.md:64), [checkpoint interpretation](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/docs/checkpoints.md:49).

### Cited Findings

- PRADA studies consecutive-query distribution alarms for DNN extraction. It motivates history-based detection but does not validate TRACE's lexical rule, LLM task populations or learned-student prevention. Tramèr et al. study functional model duplication through prediction APIs. Their evidence concerns particular models/interfaces, not a universal mapping from an alarm to economic loss. [PRADA, EuroS&P 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.02628), [Tramèr et al., USENIX Security 2016](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity16/technical-sessions/presentation/tramer).
- Distilling Step-by-Step uses teacher rationales as supervision and evaluates trained small models. Gudibande et al. show a distinction between mimicking style and transferring capability on targeted benchmarks. Together these justify measuring actual task performance and evaluating final-answer and rationale acquisition separately; they do not predict TRACE outcomes. [Hsieh et al., ACL Findings 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02301), [Gudibande et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15717).
- Carlini et al.'s production-model attack recovers a projection layer, which is a different objective from training a student on generated answers. Its reported prices must not be imported into TRACE's cost model. Adaptive-defense evaluation papers concern adversarial examples, so applying their attacker-aware methodology to TRACE is a methodological inference, not evidence of extraction prevention. [Carlini et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06634), [On Evaluating Adversarial Robustness](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06705), [On Adaptive Attacks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.08347).
- Existing TRACE protocols require fresh state, independent source roles, measured utility and matched attacker resources. The quota matcher chooses a single measured request-quota and request-plus-output-token-quota setting per baseline that matches every declared stratum within tolerance, uses development data only and never interpolates. It does not collect the grid or verify supplied losses against runs, and rate is not a supported matcher baseline. [Role separation](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/docs/evaluation.md:3), [matcher](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/quota_matching.py:9), [selection](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/quota_matching.py:73), [remaining orchestration](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/docs/evaluation.md:165).
- Teacher headroom passes only when teacher-minus-base meets the declared point margin and its family-bootstrap interval excludes zero. The clean-student gate requires positive gains excluding zero at 300 and 1,000 examples for every declared seed and pooled, with multiple seeds, compatible headroom and clean provenance. Gold supervision diagnoses learnability but cannot replace a passing clean-teacher arm. These gates prevent interpreting a weak learning setup as protection. [Headroom](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/baseline_gates.py:189), [clean gate](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/baseline_gates.py:372).
- Checkpoint students currently require a GSM8K numeric exact-match development suite, default to seeds 42/43/44, freeze target and inputs and use eligible records actually acquired at each receipt cutoff. Pending inclusion is explicit, rejected records remain excluded. The summary reports the first observed checkpoint whose mean reaches target and seed counts; unreached checkpoints are censored. It is not a confirmatory multi-domain prevention verdict. [Suite scope](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/checkpoint_learning.py:159), [plan](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/checkpoint_learning.py:226), [summary](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/checkpoint_learning.py:584).

## What can be implemented on CPU now, and what needs live hardware and data?

### Takeaway

Campaign orchestration, attribution, integrity checks and mock integration tests can be implemented now. Their scientific output is a trustworthy experiment runner. Evidence of student capability loss or a measured economic penalty requires real teacher collection, representative legitimate workloads and actual GPU training. [Fixture boundary](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/docs/campaign.md:85), [explicit student execution](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/docs/checkpoints.md:17).

### Cited Findings

- The baseline GPU workflow records hardware, pilots/evaluates the base, serves and capacity-tests the teacher, applies headroom, collects clean responses, unloads the teacher, trains and applies the clean gate. GPU steps use a shared exclusive file lock; teacher serving holds it until unload. Checkpoint execute is a separate command whose implementation invokes the runner without this shell workflow's machine lock or teacher-down check. A campaign orchestration wrapper must supply equivalent serialization. [Shared lock](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/scripts/baseline-gates.sh:96), [teacher lock](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/scripts/baseline-gates.sh:110), [workflow](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/scripts/baseline-gates.sh:163), [unload](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/scripts/baseline-gates.sh:198), [checkpoint execution](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/checkpoint_learning.py:456).
- Capacity-bound utility is optional in historical collection. Missing evidence stays unknown; valid comparisons need declared offered rate, completion floor and matching passing teacher preflight. Reduced defense completion is an outcome and must not be erased as a failing baseline. [Binding declarations](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/scripts/study.sh:97), [validity protocol](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/docs/evaluation.md:159).

### Inferences

- CPU work now: introduce explicit campaign arm manifests and fresh-state launch plans; bind config/revisions; preserve and validate campaign alert/throttle/review/entitlement/release events; classify 429 by error code; retain retry timing; repair proof/submission joins; preserve unsuccessful-attempt cost; add measured-grid collection plans and rate matching; compose checkpoint export/plan/summary with failing gates and no implicit GPU startup. Focus fixtures on a 429 restriction, proof under active cooldown, expiry/retry, review grant, overlapping late successful receipts, entitlement precedence and digest mismatch. Tests establish these contracts, not realistic detector accuracy. [API semantics](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/apps/harness-api/src/harness_api/app.py:1325), [collector gap](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/gateway_campaign.py:435), [grid scope](/home/rajveer/Projects/TRACE/packages/distillation/src/distillation/quota_matching.py:20).
