# Frontier-provider practice against unauthorized distillation

Research date 2026-10-31. Sources were fetched and relevant sections checked. Full-text verification does not establish independent access to provider telemetry. This research used the pinned TRACE audit as context only. No services, experiments, accounts, or GPU resources were touched. Source IDs match `frontier-sources.json`.

## What have frontier providers actually disclosed?

### Takeaway
The strongest current examples combine account-abuse operations with protections for hidden reasoning. They do not establish that semantic anomaly detection can identify every ordinary-answer extraction request. The October 2026 disclosures materially improve the available design references, but outcome denominators matter.

### Cited findings

- F01, Anthropic, February 23, 2026. It attributes over 16 million exchanges through about 24,000 fraudulent accounts to three labs. It discloses IP/request/infrastructure correlation, shared payments and timing, coordinated-account detection, fingerprints, reasoning-elicitation classifiers, and stronger startup/research/education verification. One proxy network managed over 20,000 accounts and mixed unrelated customers into its access pool. These are provider allegations without independent evaluation data. [Original report](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-and-preventing-distillation-attacks)
- F02, Anthropic, October 12, 2026. Controls include reasoning summarization, context/model/organization binding, two-stage classifiers, limits/bans, and billing blocked requests. It claims 10–100-fold lower reasoning tokens harvested on its own platform, under 0.1% false positives, and bans often within two hours. It acknowledges integration changes, paid false blocks, uncertain durability, and no highly sophisticated persistent circumvention attempt yet. A pretrained Claude student scored 80.4% on SWE-bench Verified using full Opus 4.8 transcripts versus 71.8% using summaries, an 8.6-point gap. This is one experiment, not proof against answer-only transfer. [Original disclosure](https://www.anthropic.com/news/distillation-defenses)
- F03, Anthropic, September 10, 2026, covering December–August. Acquisition reportedly used stolen credentials, residential proxies, disposable emails, virtual cards, routing, and purchased transcripts. Xiaomi allegedly replayed genuine user sessions through over 1,500 accounts for over 400,000 requests. Alibaba allegedly switched account pools after bans. Ordinary user traffic can therefore become training data without extraction-like content. Attribution and downstream training remain provider-reported. [Report](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)
- F04, OpenAI, September 30, 2026. It reports 16,000 patterned requests from over 4,000 users on July 24–25, explicitly attempts rather than necessarily successful extraction. A related cluster exceeded 15,000 users and was reportedly disrupted July 28. Attribution covers a core Moonshot-associated cluster, not every operator. Responses include signup/infrastructure controls, account/network enforcement, reasoning protections across identities and models, streaming holds, and partner intelligence. No measured student transfer or success denominator is published. [Original report](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-a-coordinated-model-distillation-campaign/)
- F05, Google GTIG, February 12, 2026. A reasoning-coercion campaign comprised over 100,000 prompts across non-English tasks. Google says it recognized and reduced that attack in real time. It mentions project/account disruption and proactive controls that can degrade students, but gives no mechanism or measured capability reduction. This does not establish poisoning or any particular detector. [Original report](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/distillation-experimentation-integration-ai-adversarial-use)
- F06, Anthropic, January 9, 2026. Constitutional Classifiers++ uses internal probes followed by a probe/classifier ensemble on escalated exchanges. It reports 0.05% harmless-query refusals during one month on Sonnet 4.5 traffic and approximately 1% compute overhead if applied to Opus 4.0 traffic. Over 198,000 red-team attempts produced one high-risk vulnerability. These are jailbreak/CBRN findings, not a distillation benchmark, and the populations differ. [Research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/next-generation-constitutional-classifiers)
- F07, Anthropic, October 16, 2026. Private self-assessment uses the cached conversation and feeds offline review in production. It is not the blocking classifier. Reported recall is 78% of potentially harmful and 94% of high-priority conversations at 1% FPR, against the same prompted teacher used for training. On 24 cyber tasks, inline attack success remained 54%, versus 79% unguarded and 25% with a standalone prompted classifier. More than half the organizations it surfaced lacked other detector flags in the preceding month. Teacher blind spots, evasion, and retention/privacy remain limitations. [Production technical blog](https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/self-assessment/)

### Inferences

- Explicit reasoning theft exposes an extraction action in the request/response. Passive collection of legitimate outputs may reveal only purchase, timing, identity, and campaign evidence. No cited provider solves indistinguishable passive traffic. This is an inference from scoped disclosures, not a provider theorem. [F01](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-and-preventing-distillation-attacks), [F02](https://www.anthropic.com/news/distillation-defenses), [F04](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-a-coordinated-model-distillation-campaign/)
- Harvested-token reduction is distinct from reduced student capability, campaign recall, or preventing collection through other providers. Measure acquisition and downstream transfer separately. [F02](https://www.anthropic.com/news/distillation-defenses), [F05](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/distillation-experimentation-integration-ai-adversarial-use)

### Gaps

No public feature weights, cluster algorithms, false-link rates, all thresholds, true undetected population, or controlled transfer counterfactual are available. Device fingerprinting is a plausible proposal, but these original distillation disclosures do not establish a specific implementation. No credible source here shows OpenAI or Google using MMD for this purpose. Google's student-degradation mechanism is undisclosed.

## Which controls can TRACE implement without owning inference?

### Takeaway
An opaque-teacher gateway can enforce identity-bound acquisition budgets, durable accounting, queueing, and review. Activation probes, teacher sampling changes, provider reasoning cryptography, and the same cache economics require deeper control.

### Cited findings

- F08, current Claude preserved-thinking documentation. Prefix checks include system prompt, tools, and preceding messages. Sonnet/Haiku 5.5 thinking works within the producing or a linked account, more precise than an unconditional single-account statement. Model/account mismatches can silently drop blocks without a reporting beta header. Prefix mismatches can reject or drop blocks. Test context continuity separately from HTTP success. [API docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/preserved-thinking)
- F09, current OpenAI reasoning documentation. Encrypted reasoning is returned for replay with `store:false`; retaining output items preserves context. The interface does not disclose the exact cryptographic implementation or every permitted identity transition. [Official guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning)
- F10, OpenAI safety best practices. Stable hashed user identifiers support attribution and reduce collateral organization disruption. Sessions can identify unauthenticated previews. A client-supplied identifier is not proof of a unique person. [Official guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-best-practices)
- F11, OpenAI safety-classifier docs. Streaming may be withheld while checks run; a failed check releases no tokens. High-confidence violations may block an identifier. Developers explicitly need controls against replacement accounts. Current stated policy coverage concerns biology/chemistry, so this is architectural precedent, not a public anti-distillation API claim. [Official guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-checks)
- F12, OpenAI rate-limit docs. Request/token horizons apply at organization/project rather than individual-user scope and can be shared across models. Hard spend limits stop requests; spend alerts do not. Capacity controls alone do not establish a safe acquisition ceiling. [Official guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/rate-limits)
- F13, Claude rate-limit docs. Organization token buckets coexist with workspace controls and enforced spend caps. New organizations may begin below standard limits until they establish history. Trust-dependent allowance has a production precedent. [Current docs](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/rate-limits)
- F14, Panfilov et al., August 10, 2026 preprint, HTML checked. Early-July tests demonstrated cross-session/user/model reasoning replay through weaker decoders. Proposed fixes bind identity/session/predecessor and handle legacy keys. Authors lack plaintext ground truth and note API drift. Compatibility does not prove a single global encryption key. Current exploit success cannot be inferred after mitigation. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09867v1)

### Inferences

Proposed TRACE design, not a measured production replica:

1. Persist admission and settlement state before teacher calls. Atomically reserve requests/output tokens, settle delivered output, and release unused reservations. Log attempts, refusals, upstream failures, and delivered supervision separately. Test concurrency and restart. Buffering a suspicious response is feasible but costs latency. [F11](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/safety-checks), [F12](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/rate-limits)
2. Budget at verified organization, account, and confidently linked campaign scopes. Shared NAT or public prompts should be weak review evidence, not automatic proof. Risk can trigger additional checks or reduced allowance before irreversible enforcement. This is a proposed graph policy, not a validated detector. [F01](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-and-preventing-distillation-attacks), [F03](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)
3. Retain MMD only as an auxiliary domain-conditioned shift feature. Measure incremental value over volume, output exposure, trust, and campaign grouping. Escalate to exchange classification and preserve a random audit sample to quantify the first-stage blind spot. Low probe overhead cannot be copied without inference access. [F06](https://www.anthropic.com/research/next-generation-constitutional-classifiers), [F07](https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/self-assessment/)
4. With owned inference, add protected context/identity/model bindings, probes, refusal training, and summaries. With an opaque teacher, respect upstream bindings and do not expose hidden reasoning through the wrapper. This still does not prevent learning from useful public answers. [F08](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/preserved-thinking), [F09](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning), [F14](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09867v1)

### Gaps

No source establishes a universally safe request/token quota. Thresholds need transfer learning curves, deadlines, and priced rotation. A wrapper post-response judge incurs another API call and can miss already streamed content. Current preserved-thinking docs are more precise than simplified blog statements; pin versions and test transitions.

## What are the collateral costs and credible publication opportunities?

### Takeaway
A strong research contribution would quantify acquisition and capability transfer under costly identity rotation, realistic specialists, and controlled enforcement error. Combining a content detector and quota alone is existing engineering. The deployment study must expose false links and recovery burden.

### Cited findings

- F15, Claude identity verification, August 11, 2026. Selected capabilities/integrity checks use Persona and may require physical government ID, selfie, or business information. Anthropic says data is limited to verification/compliance and offers retries and review. This imposes burden and sensitive processing, and does not establish uniqueness against stolen identities. [Help docs](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14328960-identity-verification-on-claude)
- F16, Anthropic transparency, July 23, 2026 update. H1 2026 totals are 11.4 million banned accounts, 398,000 appeals, and 42,000 overturns. About 10.6% of appeals were overturned. These aggregate counts are not distillation-specific, classifier FPR, or population false-ban estimates. [Reporting](https://www.anthropic.com/transparency/system-trust-reporting)
- F17, Claude appeals docs, retrieved October 31 and labeled updated today. Suspension/termination can be appealed. The page warns of longer-than-normal response times without promising a deadline. [Official support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8241253-safeguards-warnings-and-appeals)
- F18, Trockman/Savani personal-capacity essay, February 19, updated February 23. Antidistillation sampling is distinguished from fingerprints proving student provenance. Their argument seeks to change attacker economics while retaining openness. This is informed advocacy and a lead to research, not independent production validation. [Researchers' essay](https://antidistillation.com/blog/unexpected-externalities-of-distillation/)
- F19, anonymous Reddit thread, full content fetched October 31. An author alleges a post-verification ban before model use and disputes multiple-paid-account restrictions. Much of the post is explicitly AI-generated policy interpretation. This is only a lead on policy clarity/appeal burden, not proof of wrongful enforcement. Relative timestamps conflict with search publication labels, so exact date remains unverified. [Discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeCode/comments/1x6fc82/anthropic_is_now_limiting_having_multiple/)

### Inferences

- Candidate originality: an acquisition-control policy that minimizes maximum measured student uplift under account splitting, genuine-session replay, and an explicit legitimate-utility constraint. Report transfer versus denied specialist utility, review load, and attack dollars per capability point. Broader prior-art review must establish novelty before claiming invention. [Operational motivation F01](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-and-preventing-distillation-attacks), [F03](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)
- Separate request and account error units. A small request-level false-block rate may burden specialists over long sessions; appeal overturn fractions cannot calibrate it. [F02](https://www.anthropic.com/news/distillation-defenses), [F16](https://www.anthropic.com/transparency/system-trust-reporting)
- Offline review complements immediate controls; the same model's self-judgment should not be the only gate, and teacher agreement is not human-validated recall. [F07](https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/self-assessment/)
- Include low-and-slow specialists and replayed genuine sessions, not only stereotyped reasoning-extraction prompts. Otherwise results will overstate general protection. [F03](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026), [F04](https://openai.com/index/disrupting-a-coordinated-model-distillation-campaign/)

### Gaps

Providers give no universal acquisition threshold, staffing ratio, causal revenue trade-off, account-price distribution, or upper bound on residual student transfer. Success disclosures and anonymous failure reports are selected and financially or personally interested. Neither supplies population effectiveness. A layered design is justified, but a first-of-its-kind algorithm is not established by these sources.
