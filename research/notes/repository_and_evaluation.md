## What does TRACE actually detect and enforce today?

### Takeaway

TRACE has useful measurement, calibration, and enforcement infrastructure, but its default content detector tests query-distribution shift rather than whether someone will train a student. Its own stored audit shows that broad extraction traffic can look *less* anomalous than benign specialists. There are already lightweight extension points; the missing decisive evidence is a successful undefended student followed by an intervention that reduces its capability at acceptable legitimate-user cost.

### Cited Findings

- The encoder is pinned `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5`, CLS-pooled and normalized, with input truncation at 512 tokens. That observes the current serialized user turn, not downstream student training. [Source: encoder.py:10–35,60–76](../../packages/harness/src/harness/encoder.py).
- Default windows have 100 requests and 20 reference windows; the reference score is the average unbiased squared MMD against those references with an average of four RBF kernels at bandwidth multipliers 0.5,1,2,4. Exact duplicate prompts can be collapsed before scoring. The default horizon is 86,400 seconds with a separate 10,000-attempt cap. [Source: calibration.py:34–69](../../packages/harness/src/harness/calibration.py), [Source: mmd.py:10–34,55–92](../../packages/harness/src/harness/mmd.py).
- The rank is `p=(1+#{null scores >= current score})/(m+1)`. Default accumulation is `r_t=max(0,0.9*r_(t-1)-log(p_t)-1)`; instantaneous, e-process and reflected e-CUSUM alternatives already exist. Under the default rule, at state zero any `p>=exp(-1)` supplies no accumulating evidence. Merely changing decay cannot recover a signal whose p-values are consistently near one. [Source: calibration.py:327–340](../../packages/harness/src/harness/calibration.py), [Source: accumulators.py:13–51](../../packages/harness/src/harness/accumulators.py).
- Level never decreases within a horizon: L1 logs an alert, L2 spaces requests, L3 adds proof of work, and default L4 refuses. The 100th request can be changed by its completed window before teacher inference. A separate `verify-risk-v2` opt-in changes terminal behavior into challenges; default legacy policy still refuses. The cap and quota are independent causes, recorded as `attempt_cap`, `quota`, `rate`, or `detector`. [Source: policy.py:24–56,142–162,195–221,258–311,350–385,414–487](../../packages/harness/src/harness/policy.py).
- Durable SQLite admission/reservation/release accounting exists, including separate settlement and authoritative output-token handling. The upstream response is awaited as a complete generation. `off_path` scoring explicitly rejects durable state because queued evidence is only in process memory. [Source: app.py:294–296,735–841,880–885,931–976](../../apps/harness-api/src/harness_api/app.py).

That is a directional mismatch, not evidence that the statistic was implemented incorrectly. The saved reproduction report reports matching source hashes, almost identical references/nulls, and identical listed paper thresholds. These are inspected existing records, not rerun experiments. [Source: diagnose-detector.json, `groups.A1`](../../readings/v4/results/diagnose-detector.json), [Source: reproduce.json](../../readings/v4/results/reproduce.json).

### Inferences

- Do not rebuild a generic classifier and call it new: the repo already has a separation test. Extend its observable features and test whether it survives realistic matching. Useful candidate features are response-conditioned follow-up behavior and teacher-specific information requested, but a scripted attacker can mimic both; their value is empirical.
- Do not flip MMD to the lower tail as the proposed solution. That would classify broad coverage against the current specialist-heavy null; a legitimate broad batch and a broad extractor can be identical.
- The lowest-risk seams are `signals.py` for new cheap observables; source-disjoint `separation.py` for a feasibility gate; `fusion.py` only after calibrating whole account paths; `Policy.admit_in_transaction` for a versioned action; and buffered output before settlement for a response-projection arm. Existing store and release accounting should continue recording exactly what the client acquired.
- A protected-answer pipeline should treat output quality as an independent axis. A shorter response is not automatically a safer one: an answer-only coding solution or correct label can still be excellent training data. The primary test must train on the released output.

## Which research changes the evaluation target?

### Takeaway

Small, carefully chosen datasets can transfer valuable narrow reasoning behavior, but the result depends strongly on the student's pretrained ability and the target task. Neither a universal query limit nor a change in watermark score establishes protection. A recent lifecycle benchmark directly confirms that query detectors can recognize synthetic querying while missing ordinary-looking response imitation.

### Cited Findings

- Gudibande et al., *The False Promise of Imitating Proprietary LLMs*, 2023-05-25, arXiv v1, studies 1.5B–13B models and 0.3M–150M imitation tokens. Broad imitation often copies style more successfully than independent capability; the paper explicitly distinguishes task-specific from broad-coverage imitation. This is evidence about those bases/data, not a theorem that present-day distillation cannot work. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15717v1), saved `research/papers/PAPER_EVAL_2023_False_Promise_2305.15717v1.pdf`, fulltext checked especially sections 2–4.
- Muennighoff et al., *s1: Simple test-time scaling*, first submitted 2025-01-31, inspected v3 dated 2025-03-01, uses 1,000 curated examples and Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct with test-time budget forcing. The training cited in the paper is 26 minutes on 16 H100 GPUs. Its sample-efficiency result matters; that hardware recipe does not establish the user's GPU can reproduce the 32B result. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19393v3), saved `research/papers/PAPER_EVAL_2025_s1_2501.19393v3.pdf`, fulltext checked sections 1–3.
- Ye et al., *LIMO: Less is More for Reasoning*, first submitted 2025-02-05, latest inspected abstract v3 dated 2025-07-29, makes the important qualification that minimal demonstrations unlock knowledge already encoded in the foundation model. The v3 abstract reports 63.3% AIME24 and 95.6% MATH500; do not mix these with older v1 figures 57.1/94.8. This source was inspected at abstract/version-history level only and not downloaded. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03387v3).
- Dettmers et al., *QLoRA*, 2023-05-23 v1, trains low-rank adapters through frozen 4-bit quantized weights and demonstrates 65B finetuning on a 48 GB GPU in its setting. That establishes a memory-saving technique, not runtime or memory guarantees for an arbitrary model, context length, kernel, or GPU. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314v1), saved `research/papers/PAPER_EVAL_2023_QLoRA_2305.14314v1.pdf`, fulltext checked introduction/method and limitations.
- Liu et al., *Do Defenses Against LLM Extraction Work Across Attacks? A Lifecycle Benchmark of Black-Box Model Extraction*, first submitted 2026-09-30, inspected v2 dated 2026-10-07, compares six attacks and ten defenses. It keeps capability, fidelity, provenance, and query discrimination separate. Appendix E.4 reports MMD TPR/FPR 0/0 on SeqKD and 100/0 on QEDKS in the evaluated setting. There are 20 attack batches per method and fixed seeds, so this is illustrative narrow evidence, not population performance. Response modifications show attack-dependent effects; the authors caution that weaker provenance scores only mean evasion if a meaningful protected-versus-clean signal existed first. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00839v2), [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00839v2), saved `research/papers/PAPER_EVAL_2026_Lifecycle_Extraction_Benchmark_2610.00839v2.pdf`; inspected sections 2–4 and appendices B,D,E.

### Inferences

- The bounded target should be one valuable capability already supported by the 9B teacher but underperformed by a 1.5B–3B student. Start with GSM8K-style numeric-answer reasoning, then coding tests as a second domain. AG News can qualify plumbing cheaply but cannot substantiate broad reasoning protection.
- Base capability is a confound: an extracted student can score well even when it learned almost nothing from the teacher. Measure both teacher-minus-base headroom and clean-distilled-minus-base gain before testing a defense.
- For output projection, compare the same underlying question with full correct teacher explanation, a compact verified solution, and final-answer-only supervision. This asks whether excess reasoning detail is responsible for measurable transfer while preserving the intended legitimate task. It does not pretend every task has a discardable explanation.

### Gaps

- None of the cited work proves universal prevention of useful-answer distillation from identical observable traffic. Better pretrained students, synthetic augmentation and alternative training losses may undo a measured suppression.
- The recent benchmark is a preprint. Its narrow models/tasks, fixed seeds and batch sample size limit its evidentiary strength; we did not reproduce it.

## What can be implemented and falsified within days or two weeks?

### Takeaway

A minimum credible study is possible using a pinned roughly 2B student, cached teacher transcripts, lightweight detector features and a few LoRA training runs. First demonstrate positive transfer. Then test a specific content-selective gate or response transformation against adaptive attacks at matched legitimate utility. Stop if the proposed detector only learns volume/source or the output change merely shortens data without reducing matched-token transfer.

### Cited Findings

- The existing runner already pins Qwen3.5-2B-Base and a Qwen3.5-9B tokenizer revision, uses rank-16 LoRA, BF16, completion-only SFT, gradient checkpointing and three epochs. However, it is explicitly AG News only, loads the full BF16 model, assumes a Qwen3.5 class, hardcodes 16 generated evaluation tokens, and rejects overlength examples rather than truncating them. Extending to math/code requires a versioned task recipe and longer generation budget; adding 4-bit loading is a separate QLoRA extension, not a flag that exists today. [Source: learning_curve.py:15–40](../../packages/distillation/src/distillation/learning_curve.py), [Source: learning_curve_runner.py:14–141](../../packages/distillation/src/distillation/learning_curve_runner.py).
- The current report distinguishes unknown released-token totals and pending outcomes instead of treating missing values as zero. Preserve that logic, but note `campaign_report` itself says admission is not recorded for every attempt and its restriction coverage excludes delayed serves. Ground primary metrics in the durable gateway ledger, then reconcile campaign telemetry. [Source: evaluation_protocol.py:19–78](../../packages/distillation/src/distillation/evaluation_protocol.py).

### Inferences

The following is a proposed protocol, not an established result or promised runtime.

1. **Day 1: establish feasibility and prevent a meaningless experiment.** Confirm GPU model/VRAM and library versions without teacher traffic. Pin the existing 2B student, choose a fresh source-disjoint exploratory math suite, and evaluate frozen base and teacher on identical held-out problems. Use 300–500 problems for an initial precision check; reserve a separate confirmatory set. Teacher accuracy should exceed base by a prespecified practically meaningful gap, proposed 10 percentage points. If the confidence interval includes zero or the gap is too small, change the domain/base before testing defense. The threshold is a study choice, not a mathematical constant.
2. **Days 1–2: collect once, serialize GPU work.** Obtain 1,000 fresh task-specific teacher responses and optionally 1,000 matched benign sessions. Freeze generation settings, task-family provenance and token counts. For offline response transformations, reuse one clean transcript and cache transformed outputs; do not simultaneously serve the teacher and train the student on the single GPU. Any synthetic explanation rewrite must be validated for correctness. Keep teacher collection outside the detector-only computation budget.
3. **Days 2–3: qualify positive transfer.** Train the existing 2B LoRA recipe on 300 and 1,000 clean examples, starting each from the pinned base. Use a small pilot to measure steps/second, peak VRAM, evaluation cost and one-epoch learning before committing the full schedule. Prefer BF16 LoRA if it fits; use QLoRA only if needed and verify that its clean-transfer baseline still works. If the clean distilled student has no reproducible gain over base, conclude that this pair/budget cannot test protection. Do not celebrate that as defense success.
4. **Days 3–5: isolate the causal comparison.** Train fresh students on: (a) clean full responses, (b) proposed protected responses, (c) clean responses with the same total released training tokens as (b), and (d) protected responses after attacker-side cleanup or paraphrasing. Add a gold-answer or trusted public-data student as a diagnostic for training capacity. Start with one seed for screening; one seed is development evidence only. For a query gate, first run the detector offline on fixed streams and train on its actually accepted examples; compare against a request quota and request-plus-token quota calibrated to the same benign utility loss.
5. **Days 5–7: one honest pilot verdict.** Repeat promising comparisons for three training seeds and evaluate by paired task-family resampling. Train a final-answer-only attacker and an attacker asking for short correct solutions, since the main projection hypothesis is about excess reasoning. Hold out an entire attacker family during detector fitting. If the candidate fails the adversarial controls below, document the negative result and stop tuning it on the test set.
6. **Days 8–14: confirm only the surviving narrow claim.** Add one coding domain with sandboxed tests or a second math source family, a fresh confirmatory partition, five training seeds if throughput permits, and account/session replay through the real buffered gateway. Measure pre-release timing, false-positive harm and teacher capacity under the declared rate. This stage supports a narrow empirical defense claim if it succeeds; it still does not prove general prevention.

Minimum adversarial controls:

- **Exact matched-observable replay:** assign the same prompts, timestamps, account metadata and request history to a legitimate user and an extractor who trains afterward. Their detector scores must match. Treat any claimed discrimination as leakage. The correct result establishes the observability limit.
- **Matched-source benign automation:** educators generating worked solutions, coding CI agents, evaluation teams and enterprise batch jobs should match attacker domain, source, token length, mixture, request count and rate. Remove count/rate features in the main content-only ablation; otherwise a learned quota can masquerade as intent detection.
- **Adaptive query appearance:** topic-sorted extraction, natural conversation wrappers, paraphrases, low-and-slow collection, duplicated benign turns, and multiple accounts. Preserve attacker utility: success means those outputs still train a useful student, not merely that p-values increased.
- **Adaptive released-output use:** final-answer parsing, rationale stripping, semantically preserving paraphrase, and a stronger pretrained student in the two-week extension. Do not use a large rewriter in the default defense if the user's GPU cannot serve it; any rewriter's cost counts.

Primary measurement definitions:

Let `A_T`, `A_B`, `A_C`, and `A_D` denote held-out objective task accuracy for teacher, untrained student base, student trained on clean collected output, and student trained on protected output. Report all four, uncertainty, and per-task values. Define normalized transfer `R_C=(A_C-A_B)/(A_T-A_B)` and `R_D=(A_D-A_B)/(A_T-A_B)`, with untruncated values permitted because students can exceed teachers. Only use these ratios when the teacher-base denominator is positive, large enough and statistically stable; otherwise report raw differences and mark normalized transfer undefined. Define transfer suppression `S=1-(A_D-A_B)/(A_C-A_B)` only when clean gain is convincingly positive. Negative or greater-than-one values are data, not quantities to silently clip. Confidence intervals must include denominator uncertainty, for example through paired task-family bootstrap; training-seed uncertainty must be reported separately.

For detection, use horizon-level `FPR=P(any unjustified intervention within a complete benign session/horizon)`, not per-window FPR alone. Report restricted benign responses, legitimate task-completion accuracy, release-token cost, explanation adequacy, challenge burden and p50/p95 latency by role/domain/language/volume stratum. Five hundred benign horizons with zero false positives only gives an approximate 95% upper bound near 0.6%; roughly 300 zero-event horizons are needed for an upper bound near 1%, and each claimed stratum needs its own evidence. Sparse strata should abstain rather than inherit a pooled guarantee.

For enforcement, report first alert, first restriction and first withheld response separately; detector-triggered refusals separately from attempt caps, quota, rate and upstream failure; released distinct examples and authoritative delivered tokens before intervention; and restriction overshoot from in-flight work. A response delayed past the campaign deadline is a deadline-specific restriction, not destruction of training information. A challenge solved successfully still adds cost but does not remove its released label.

Budget matching requires two distinct experiments. Equal-query comparison fixes question identities and count, allowing output lengths to differ. Equal-token comparison fixes the student's total teacher-delivered supervision tokens, permitting additional examples from the same source distribution for shorter outputs and counting their extra queries, money and wall time. Equal training steps alone does not equalize information. Also include clean truncated or clean subsampled controls so a projection is not credited merely for delivering fewer tokens. Fix total attacker compute separately; report an adaptive-compute extension rather than silently granting one arm more training.

Calibration must split source families, accounts and time into fitting, score calibration, policy calibration and locked evaluation roles. Do not split individual windows from the same account across train and test. If repeated looks use fixed calibration scores, their ranks need not be conditionally superuniform; multiplying their nominal e-values does not establish a Ville bound. For a practical first study, freeze an account-horizon trajectory score and calibrate the maximum over the entire prescribed path on independent benign horizons; evaluate the fixed stopping policy once. Changing the horizon, look schedule, feature weights or response policy requires new calibration. Empirical whole-horizon calibration is a controlled finite-study claim, not a universal anytime theorem under arbitrary drift.

Proposed continuation criterion: retain a candidate only if it lowers protected-student accuracy versus the strongest matched clean baseline by a practically relevant margin, proposed 5 percentage points or 25% of clean transfer gain, with acceptable uncertainty; keeps benign task success loss within a prespecified budget, proposed 2 percentage points overall and an explicit specialist allowance; and survives at least one held-out adaptive querying/rewrite strategy. These are suggested operational thresholds to preregister, not published guarantees. A result requiring major legitimate explanation degradation should be called a product tradeoff, not free protection.

Abandon or narrow the candidate when matched benign automation is restricted at similar rates to extraction; its benefit disappears after count/rate/source matching; clean transfer is absent; equal-token or answer-only students recover the original gain; paraphrasing recovers capability; or a useful student is already trained before the first intervention. A failed detector can still support a precise observability result. A successful projection on one math task can support only that measured task/model/budget claim.

### Gaps

- The 3–7 day schedule depends on measured collection and training throughput. The repository's existing `estimated_gpu_hours=[4,7]` is planning metadata tested by a CPU fixture, not a benchmark on the user's card. Long CoT or coding contexts may change activation memory and evaluation time by large factors.
- Natural legitimate traffic and realistic account-link evidence are not established by synthetic wrappers. A source-matched synthetic study is a development gate; population false-positive claims need an appropriately sampled legitimate workload.
- No new training, teacher requests, code changes, or deployment occurred in this audit. Only these research notes, extracted paper text, the paper manifest, and four new PDFs, now in `research/papers`, were created.
