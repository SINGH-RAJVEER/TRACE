## Which output defenses offer actual protection?

### Takeaway

Control the amount and kind of supervision released before relying on output poisoning. Watermarks can provide evidence after training; they do not, by themselves, prevent learning. The strongest recent antidistillation proposal has meaningful adaptive counterevidence, so it belongs in a research arm rather than as the basis of a production guarantee.

### Cited Findings

- Antidistillation sampling, ADS, adjusts teacher token probabilities using a proxy model's estimated downstream loss. The implementation precomputes a gradient and uses two perturbed-proxy forward passes for each generated token. The main setup uses a DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B teacher, Qwen2.5-3B proxy and Llama-3.2-3B student. Figure 3 reports a setting with teacher accuracy around 70% while the student falls below its undistilled baseline. These are small-model reasoning experiments, not measured protection of a frontier production model. [Savani et al., 2025, full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.13146v5).
- The direct counterstudy reproduces the model family and fixes a GSM8K data pool of 5,231 prompts. Free repetition deletion recovers much of the reported protection. For the three lowest perturbation strengths, doubling the query budget nearly recovers temperature-sampling student performance. A one-token assistant prefill improves the attacker further. Its matched-utility-budget curve is interpolated, not a separately measured sweep. The authors distinguish query budget, data budget, and interface profile. This is the most relevant recent falsification test for an ADS-based proposal. [Libon et al., 2026, July v2, full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.25059v2).
- Prediction Poisoning changes classifier probability outputs to damage the attacker's training objective. Its original author page reports up to 85 times attacker error in selected image-classification experiments. That maximum is not a general effect size and does not transfer to free-form LLM answers. [Orekondy, Schiele and Fritz, ICLR 2020](https://resources.mpi-inf.mpg.de/d2/orekondy/predpoison/).
- Adaptive Misinformation selectively supplies incorrect predictions on detected out-of-distribution inputs. Its reported trade-off depends on its attacks using such inputs. The paper reports clone degradation up to 40% and benign accuracy loss below 0.5% in its setting; neither statement demonstrates detection of same-distribution extraction. [Kariyappa and Qureshi, CVPR 2020](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Kariyappa_Defending_Against_Model_Stealing_Attacks_With_Adaptive_Misinformation_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf).
- ADS-C is a July 2026 preprint for probability-vector classifiers. It keeps every served top-1 label unchanged and reports soft-target student losses of 17.4 percentage points on CIFAR-100, 29.6 on CIFAR-10 and 13.3 on Tiny-ImageNet in its experiments. Its own abstract explains the hard-label fallback. Since TRACE's AG News channel already returns a label, perturbing hidden probabilities while preserving that label leaves this channel unchanged. [Ullah and Khojasteh, 2026, abstract verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15467).
- Antidistillation fingerprinting, ADFP, optimizes a watermark's expected absorption by a proxy student. It evaluates GSM8K, OASST1 and MBPP, including open-weight and sampled-token access to suspect models. Its closed-weight test still needs controlled continuations for supplied contexts, which is a stronger capability than unrestricted chat access alone. Its evaluation reuses training prompts with new generations, so it does not establish completely prompt-disjoint attribution. The paper's stated ICML 2026 status is metadata, not independently checked proceedings acceptance. [Xu et al., 2026, May v2, full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03812v2).
- Radioactive watermarking explicitly asks whether a subsequent model learned from marked text. The paper reports high-confidence detection with 5% watermarked training text in an open-weight setting. It is evidence for attribution under stated access and data assumptions, not a 95% prevention rate. [Sander et al., NeurIPS 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14904). [Official reproduction code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/radioactive-watermark).
- GINSW, called GINSEW in some PDF material, injects decoding-distribution signals and probes a suspect model. The proceedings abstract reports a 19 to 29 point mAP advantage over earlier watermark-removal baselines. This measures suspect identification rather than suppressed student capability. [Zhao, Wang and Li, ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/zhao23i.html).
- DAWN changes a small keyed subset of API labels to leave a learned signature. It reports 0.03 to 0.5% prediction-accuracy loss in the evaluated classifiers. It predates TRACE's keyed deception, which is explicitly modeled on DAWN and Adaptive Misinformation. Its public code warns that extraction-attack experiments are omitted. [Szyller et al., ACM MM 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.00830). [Official code](https://github.com/ssg-research/dawn-dynamic-adversarial-watermarking-of-neural-networks).
- SynthID-Text integrates a watermark into sampling, including speculative sampling. Its production experiment assessed feedback on nearly 20 million Gemini responses. That demonstrates scale and a quality evaluation; it does not demonstrate distillation prevention or identify every copied student. [Dathathri et al., Nature 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08025-4).
- PostMark can watermark already generated text without teacher logits. It inserts input-dependent words and reports a quality/robustness trade-off. It is suitable as a wrapper experiment for long prose, but arbitrary word insertion is a poor fit for exact labels, short spans and executable code. That last judgment is an engineering inference. [Chang et al., EMNLP 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.506/).
- Watermark Stealing shows that querying a marked API can support both spoofing and removal; its reported attacks cost under $50 with average success above 80% for the schemes tested. A watermark accusation therefore needs clean controls and attack-specific confidence analysis. [Jovanovic, Staab and Vechev, ICML 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/jovanovic24a.html).
- Paraphrase results depend on watermark, paraphraser and token count. One reliability study reports detection after roughly 800 tokens on average following adversarial human paraphrasing at a false-positive target of 1e-5. This says little about a one-token classifier response. [Kirchenbauer et al., ICLR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04634). DIPPER demonstrates evasion and a provider-side retrieval alternative, with privacy and storage implications. [Krishna et al., NeurIPS 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13408).
- WASH is a May 2026 preprint that averages distributions across models to weaken watermarks. Its theory assumes bounded, independent, centered perturbations relative to a common consensus distribution. Its practical algorithm addresses vocabulary differences. It supplies an adaptive mixing test, not a proof that every watermark or text-only interface is defeated. [Wu et al., 2026, full-text assumptions checked](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30501v1).
- Rationales can improve supervision efficiency. Distilling Step-by-Step reports gains using rationales across four NLP benchmarks. [Hsieh et al., Findings ACL 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02301). Stronger base models and task-specific data also change the threat: DeepSeek's official code describes distilling six student models on 800,000 curated examples. [DeepSeek-AI, 2025](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1). Earlier imitation research found style transfer without general capability transfer in its 2023 setting, so human preference alone is an inadequate extraction outcome. [Gudibande et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15717).
- Richer numerical interfaces can leak model structure as well as supervision. A 2024 study extracts a production model's output projection layer using numerical API behavior. The problem differs from semantic capability distillation and needs separate interface tests. [Carlini et al., ICML 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.06634).

### Inferences

| Intervention | Wrapper with text access | Sampler/logit access | Weight/training access | What it can establish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Request, output-token, concurrency and time budgets | Yes | No | No | Bound released material for linked identities |
| Remove exposed probabilities and redundant traces | Yes, if endpoint supports it | Optional | No | Reduces channels; label-only extraction remains |
| Final-answer or compact-answer tier | Yes | Optional | No | A testable supervision/utility trade-off, not a guarantee |
| Keyed wrong responses already in TRACE | Yes | No | No | Research-only corruption/attribution; false positives damage legitimate answers |
| PostMark and other post-hoc rewriting | Yes, with another generation pass | No | No | Text provenance under tested attacks, with quality/latency cost |
| SynthID-style sampling mark | No | Yes | No | Provenance of output text; student attribution needs a separate protocol |
| ADS | No | Yes, plus proxy computation | Proxy weights/gradient | Lower distillability in tested regimes; adaptivity weakens evidence |
| ADFP | No | Yes, plus proxy distribution | Proxy weights | Evidence of absorption under a declared suspect-access model |
| Train teacher to hide transferable skills | No | Insufficient alone | Yes | Research hypothesis with substantial utility risk |

Randomizing language, changing temperature, serving a weaker model, or mixing teachers should not be sold as security without student experiments. Randomness may be averaged, incorrect examples may be filtered, and easier outputs from a weaker teacher can still teach useful skills. If a four-label classifier preserves its correct top label, that correct label remains trainable supervision. Changing the label buys reduced learning at an immediate accuracy cost.

Private reasoning need not be exposed merely because a customer needs an answer. However, restricting reasoning removes value for users who legitimately need explanations and does not prevent learning from final answers, demonstrations or code. Measure the trade-off by task and client type.

### Gaps

No public evidence found here establishes a general output-only method that preserves normal frontier-model utility while preventing a capable adaptive student from learning. No live TRACE capability measurements were run. Independent reproduction of the 2026 preprints remains necessary. Proprietary companies may have unpublished methods; no architecture below claims to reconstruct their secrets.

## What should TRACE evolve into, and when is a rebuild justified?

### Takeaway

Keep the existing research software, repair its measurement and state boundaries, and make supervision acquisition the primary controlled quantity. A provider rebuild is justified only if you own inference serving and have enough legitimate traffic to validate the added account and output controls.

### Cited Findings

- The inspected code already implements `e_process` and `e_cusum`; the comments correctly condition the e-process guarantee on valid conditional p-values. Replacing the accumulator without validating calibration does not create that guarantee. [Local code](../../packages/harness/src/harness/accumulators.py).
- `AggregateMonitor` explicitly raises alerts without changing individual levels. Global traffic shifts do not identify the guilty principal. The archived shared-gateway study explicitly disables aggregate monitoring. [Monitor](../../packages/harness/src/harness/monitor.py); current-main historical path is `legacy/packages/distillation/src/distillation/gateway_study.py`.
- Keyed deception is deterministic per canonical request and shared across accounts, but begins only after L4. It cannot reduce pre-escalation acquisition. Coding deception is prohibited. The shared-gateway historical study permits refusal only. [Deception](../../packages/harness/src/harness/deception.py); [policy](../../packages/harness/src/harness/policy.py); [API](../../apps/harness-api/src/harness_api/app.py).
- Account state is in a process dictionary; proofs and pending admissions are also in process memory. Decision logging occurs at admission before upstream delivery. [Policy state](../../packages/harness/src/harness/policy.py); [API admission and release](../../apps/harness-api/src/harness_api/app.py). The audit independently reproduced 135 logged window p-values, but its inspected test data is now development evidence and cannot become a fresh final test. [Audit](../../readings/v4/REPORT.md).

### Inferences

#### Evolutionary TRACE design, proposed

Request flow: authenticate principal and entitlement, canonicalize and validate, reserve request and maximum output cost transactionally, evaluate cheap behavioral policy, optionally compute content evidence, choose output tier, invoke teacher, stream under the reservation, settle delivered usage, append a completion event, update longer-term monitoring.

1. Keep quota, rate, full MMD and instantaneous MMD as reproducible baselines. Add request-plus-output-token quotas, per-identity concurrency caps, and cumulative verified-principal budgets. Token limits complement request limits because a code solution and a class label have different supervision density. Tokens are an accounting unit, not a proven information measure.
2. Put one admission ledger behind all workers. Use a transaction for reserve/check/debit rather than independent read-then-write counters. Persist principal, horizon, policy revision, accumulated evidence, request binding, challenge redemption and idempotency key. Reserve the declared output maximum, refund unused capacity after confirmed completion, and charge any delivered prefix after cancellation. Reconciliation must not refund a client disconnect that already received supervision. A network write is an upper-bound proxy for delivery, not proof of receipt.
3. Keep attempt accounting separate from released-supervision accounting. Count retries and proof submissions as load, but do not pretend a rejected proof released a new label. Same idempotency key must not regenerate fresh samples; bind it to the entire normalized request and output settings. Cache reuse also has privacy boundaries between tenants.
4. Fix the topic truncation bug in new workloads. Build domain-conditional and client-profile score baselines. Include legitimate specialists, generalists, batch evaluation, nightly automation and shared enterprise keys. Recalibrate every changed feature, window, embedding, tail test and policy. Keep MMD in alert mode until it beats quota/rate at matched legitimate-user utility on unseen sources.
5. Use a low-friction verification or review path before irreversible restrictions for uncertain detections. Verified enterprise automation should receive explicit contractual budgets. Show the user which limit was hit and how to appeal. Preserve a mechanism to correct mistakenly linked accounts and restore their budgets without deleting the audit trail.
6. Add optional answer-only versus explanation tiers only for tasks with a measurable legitimate need for each. Do not degrade code, arithmetic or factual claims with hidden noise in normal production. Keep deception as a consented laboratory arm with labels in researcher telemetry.
7. Separate detection from attribution. An output-watermark experiment can run independently of MMD, but must report the suspect access, number of probes, negative controls and multiple-comparison adjustment. Attribution alone never reduces the bytes already released.

#### Provider rebuild, proposed

Use an account service and durable quota ledger before the inference router. A cheap synchronous policy controls principal-level volume, concurrency and permitted output channels. An asynchronous analysis service consumes privacy-reduced events and produces risk evidence at the organization or campaign level. An enforcement service applies time-bounded controls with a review reason and policy version. The model-serving tier optionally controls raw reasoning exposure, numerical channels, sampler fingerprints and research-only ADS. The output gateway reserves and meters every streamed prefix. A separate attribution service evaluates suspect models using registered secret keys and clean controls.

Cross-account linkage should use strong first-party relationships first, such as organization ownership or verified payment entity, with explicit collision and appeal tests. IP address and semantic similarity alone are insufficient to deny service to university networks or shared corporate users. As a practical research wrapper, TRACE cannot obtain hidden provider identity signals from an upstream API, and it cannot enforce anything after the attacker calls that upstream directly.

Deploy the content feature pipeline in shadow mode, then cap only high-confidence linked campaigns. Do not block all accounts contributing to a global MMD alert. Keep asynchronous analysis out of the model-serving critical path, but disclose the exposure bound: at acquisition rate R and analysis/enforcement delay L, a campaign can release roughly R times L extra units, plus already reserved in-flight capacity. The accounting ledger, not the detector, is what makes that overshoot measurable.

#### Cost model and engineering budgets, all estimates

Use measured p50/p95/p99 admission latency, time to first token, token throughput and total GPU-seconds, alongside successful tasks per dollar. Proposed initial engineering limits are at most 10 ms added p95 admission latency and at most 5% end-to-end latency overhead for the wrapper. These are targets to test, not published results.

Durable accounting adds one reservation and one settlement operation per request; pipelined or batched processing can change cost but must preserve admission atomicity. Metadata processing is approximately linear in requests. BGE adds one encoder pass per embedded prompt and is currently serialized inside the encoder instance. Kernel scoring costs grow with window size, reference count and embedding width; amortized time can conceal a large completion-window tail. Measure long inputs and cold caches.

An ADS token costs one teacher pass plus two proxy passes. A crude dense-parameter FLOP estimate for a 7B teacher with two 3B proxies is 13/7, about 1.86 times baseline per token before caching and batching effects. This is an analyst estimate, not measured wall time. Two proxy caches and weights add memory. Frontier MoE active parameters, communication, quantization and speculative decoding can make that ratio misleading. ADFP's explicit score needs a proxy distribution but not ADS's two perturbed copies, so benchmark it separately. Post-hoc rewriting adds an additional generation over the answer and usually delays release if quality must be checked first. Watermark sampling without another network pass can be much cheaper, as the SynthID production evidence suggests.

Store policy counters, keyed prompt digests and compact features by default. Retain full prompts/answers only in a restricted, short-lived investigation store when needed, with stated purpose, access logs and deletion rules. Plain SHA-256 hashes permit dictionary matching of common prompts; keyed hashes reduce that exposure but still link behavior. Embeddings can retain sensitive information and are not automatically anonymized. Cross-account investigations and fingerprint ownership claims should have separate permissions and evidence standards.

### Gaps

The appropriate legitimate-client quotas, useful explanation tiers, rate ceilings and identity-linkage precision are unknown until observed on representative traffic. A prototype on synthetic accounts cannot establish public-provider false-positive rates. No CPU timing, GPU timing, database sizing or production throughput was measured in this task.

## What original contribution is defensible, and how should it be tested?

### Takeaway

A new detector ensemble is unlikely to be the strongest contribution. The more defensible project is a tightly scoped study of acquisition control, adaptive distillation and legitimate utility, with one tested mechanism beyond the existing literature. Negative results are publishable evidence if the comparison and data separation are sound, though acceptance cannot be promised.

### Cited Findings

The proposed originality must be compared with ADS, DAWN, radioactive watermarking, ADFP and the query/data/interface framework, rather than claiming these ideas anew. [ADS](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.13146v5); [DAWN](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.00830); [radioactivity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14904); [ADFP](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.03812v2); [Libon et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.25059v2). Existing TRACE token-matched training controls in the archived `GatewayStudy.controls` are also prior implementation, not a new idea.

### Inferences

#### Originality ledger, proposed research options

| Candidate contribution | Closest prior art | What might actually be new | What would defeat the claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Utility-constrained acquisition policy across heterogeneous tasks | Quotas/rate controls; TRACE token-matched controls; Libon query/data budgets | A specified optimization that allocates request, output and explanation budgets by verified use case, evaluated for minimum attacker cost to reach a capability target while preserving specialists | Prior work already optimizes the same objective under the same interface and adaptive threat model; a plain weighted token bucket is engineering only |
| Adaptive composition of acquisition limits and output interventions | ADS and its 2026 reevaluation; ADFP; keyed misinformation | A factorial causal measurement of whether quotas stop cheap cleanup/retry/mixing adaptations, and whether gains exceed matched fewer-output controls under matched legitimate solved-task cost | Same interaction already studied; any apparent protection vanishes when attacker compute, retries and missing-data selection are matched |
| Restart-safe streaming acquisition guarantees with empirical learning curves | Durable quotas/idempotency; request-release accounting | A formal bound on released prefixes under crashes, retries and concurrent account linking, paired with an honest empirical capability-risk envelope and an open benchmark of failures | Calling durable accounting alone a novel ML defense; claiming the token bound proves a capability bound; existing systems already provide the same formal guarantee |

Best capstone direction: start with the first candidate and use the third as a systems contribution. Make the title about measured acquisition risk under legitimate utility constraints rather than preventing all distillation. Consider the second only if inference/sampler access and enough training budget exist. A systematic source-disjoint counterevaluation of current TRACE plus public output defenses is also defensible as an empirical contribution; it is not a novel defense algorithm.

#### Frozen study design

1. Declare three independent attacker budgets: online queries/output tokens/money; available distinct prompts and outside data; offline compute. Declare the interface, including logits, answer-only modes, prefill, tools, attachments, multi-turn history, reasoning summaries and streaming. Declare account cost and available verified identities separately. Refusing logprobs does not preclude hard-label or text distillation.
2. Divide by source family and role before computing features. Fitting sources build representations; score-calibration sources fit nulls; policy-development sources select thresholds and budgets; final benign and final attack sources remain locked. Group SQuAD by document/title, coding by repository/problem lineage, math by generator/problem family, and news by publisher/time/topic. Near-duplicate screening must precede allocation. Existing audit probes, A1/A2 schedules and all inspected test outputs are development-only.
3. Use independent legitimate accounts for low-volume casual use, topic specialists, multilingual use, batch research/evaluation, shared organizations and repeat-heavy applications. Interleave them with campaigns under a teacher capacity that meets the undefended deadline baseline. Preserve deliberate overload experiments separately. Account-level restrictions and completed-task utility must both be measured.
4. Compare allow, existing full MMD, instantaneous MMD, request quota, rate limit, request-plus-output-token quota, domain-conditional alert plus quota, and the proposed policy. For output interventions add only a small frozen factorial subset: none, compact answers, sampler fingerprint if available, ADS if available. Compare the same online budget and deadline, then a second panel at matched benign solved-task cost. Report every escalation level separately from withheld output.
5. Train students at acquisition checkpoints, suggested 100, 300, 1,000, 3,000, 6,000 and 12,000 responses, with measured released tokens alongside each point. Include the untrained base, gold-supervision and undefended-teacher controls. Include at least two student families and three training seeds at the final selected conditions. Use task-specific accuracy/execution/F1 and far-transfer tests; do not substitute writing style or judge preference for capability.
6. Adaptive attackers must include topic reordering, human-like bursts, account splitting/rotation, cover traffic, few-shot batching, extracting only final labels, requesting explanations in other formats, free repetition cleanup, verifier filtering, selective retries, alternative-teacher mixing, paraphrasing/backtranslation and fingerprint removal. Test assistant prefill when the deployed interface permits it; test user-message prefix instructions separately rather than pretending they are equivalent. Charge extra online calls and paraphraser compute consistently.
7. Report released distinct examples, responses and output tokens before first restriction; total acquired by deadline; campaign time and spend to each student capability target; benign completed correct tasks by client type; total and scored account escalation rates; provider overhead and review workload. Closed-teacher capability emulation and literal weight extraction are distinct endpoints.
8. Use paired comparisons by campaign/source family and cluster bootstrap at the source family or organization level. Training seeds are conditional variability, not independent users. Do not bootstrap correlated windows as independent. Report individual campaigns when source-family count is too small for stable intervals. Adjust for prespecified multiple comparisons or limit confirmatory hypotheses.

#### Proposed acceptance and rejection criteria

- Utility gate: no more than a predeclared 1 percentage point decrease in successful legitimate task completion in each high-priority client group, and no more than 1% benign horizons restricted. These are design goals requiring stakeholder selection. At zero events, roughly 300 independent benign horizons are needed for a one-sided 95% upper bound near 1%; correlated synthetic accounts do not meet that independence condition.
- Security gate: at least double the attacker spend or time needed to reach a preregistered capability threshold, with a source-clustered confidence interval excluding no improvement, under the strongest declared adaptive attack. Choose the threshold from development learning curves, for example recovering half the teacher-over-base gain, and freeze it before final testing.
- Mechanism gate: an output or detector intervention must beat the strongest matched request/token/rate baseline at matched benign task utility. If the entire student reduction is explained by fewer released examples, claim acquisition control only. That remains useful but does not establish poisoning or statistical detection value.
- Systems gate: crashes, duplicate proofs, retries, cancellation, clock changes, multiple workers and concurrent near-limit requests must never release beyond the stated reservation bound. Tests should prove failures cannot silently refresh the horizon. Actual delivered-prefix uncertainty must be explicit.
- Attribution gate: independently generated clean models, mixed-source students, clean fine-tunes, key-rotation controls and spoofing attacks must meet a declared accusation-level false-positive target. A low p-value from one key or one model family is insufficient. Report the access needed to reach that confidence.
- Reject a new detector as a production restriction trigger if it cannot beat budgets for legitimate specialists or loses all benefit after topic reordering. Reject a fingerprint as prevention if student capability is unchanged. Reject ADS for deployment if its matched-utility gain disappears under cleanup, retries or permitted input controls.

#### Phases and estimated resources, not measurements

Phase A, one to three researcher days: provenance and split audit, protocol, offline replay, accounting design, no teacher GPU. Preserve the frozen historical study. Check at least several hundred benign account horizons before claiming a 1% restriction target, while acknowledging real-data coverage remains harder than sample count.

Phase B, two to five days: isolated wrapper prototype and failure injection on a fake teacher, then a small 100-prompt generation pilot on reserved compute. Measure actual token throughput and training speed before funding a sweep. An initial output pilot of 6,000 examples times 1,024 generated tokens is about 6.14 million teacher tokens per condition. At a measured aggregate 50 tokens/second, this alone is about 34 hours per condition; at 200 tokens/second, about 8.5 hours. ADS proxy passes and long tails add cost. These rates are illustrative inputs, not hardware promises.

Phase C, one to three weeks given reserved capacity: evaluate a narrowed student matrix. Four selected conditions times two student families times three training seeds is 24 fits at one acquisition point; four acquisition points make 96 fits. An illustrative 2 to 8 GPU-hours per fit implies 192 to 768 GPU-hours, before teacher generation, evaluations, reruns and development. LoRA on 1.5B to 3B students and a 7B teacher may fit suitable 24 to 80 GB accelerator configurations depending on sequence length, precision and serving layout; benchmark rather than promise a specific card. Full ADS reproduction needs teacher and two proxy configurations plus caches, so schedule separate resources.

Phase D, two or more weeks: held-out simultaneous benign/attack runs, deployment shadow measurements and appeals review. A student checkpoint cannot be recovered retrospectively from logged p-values. Inference-output comparisons also cannot establish downstream learning without actually training and evaluating students.

### Gaps

Originality requires a second literature search around the selected narrow objective before manuscript claims. Neither absence from this survey nor a new combination of named defenses proves novelty. Compute availability, legitimate traffic access, production entitlements, and calibrated teacher-to-student transfer remain unknown. No new experiments or code changes were made by this research task.
