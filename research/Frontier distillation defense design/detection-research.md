# Statistical and behavioral defenses against distillation

## What can a detector ensemble establish, and where does it fail?

### Takeaway

Build a bounded-acquisition service with an evidence-producing detector ensemble, rather than treating anomaly detection as proof of extraction. The strongest direct counterexample to a universal query detector is now external as well as local: the September 2026 lifecycle benchmark reports MMD detecting 100% of QEDKS batches and 0% of fixed-pool SeqKD batches under the same evaluation procedure. Detection concerns observable query construction, not the downstream intent to train a student. [D01](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00839v1)

### Cited Findings

Sources checked 2026-10-31. Full-text verified means the paper was accessible and relevant sections checked, not independently reproduced. Peer review and the inspected version are distinct. The JSON supplies metadata. Avoid conflating batch TPR with campaign blocking, or zero observed false positives with a certified low population FPR.

- D01, Liu et al., *Do Defenses Against LLM Extraction Work Across Attacks? A Lifecycle Benchmark of Black-Box Model Extraction*, arXiv v1 submitted September 30 2026. Full text verified. Six attacks, ten defenses and two rewriting adaptations. Table 12 uses 20 attack batches and only four benign test batches, 50 queries per batch. MMD and SEAT detect QEDKS at 100%, while MMD detects SeqKD at 0% and SEAT at 5%. Descriptive single-seed results with adapted detector protocols, not original-defense reproductions or evidence for 1% FPR. Separates capability, fidelity and provenance, making it close prior art for a lifecycle benchmark. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00839v1)
- D02, Liu, Guo and Dong, *An Embarrassingly Simple Detector for Model Extraction Attacks in Large Language Model API Traffic*, June 4 2026 arXiv v1. Full text verified. Fourteen attacker-normal pairs, three seeds, 1,500-query windows, normalized BGE-small embeddings and 20 RBF-MMD reference comparisons. Reports 0.3% benign FPR, 100% pure-attacker TPR and 90.5% mean TPR over contamination fractions. Appendix B warns about semantic proximity, 5% contamination, benign drift and lack of account-aware monitoring. That protocol differs sharply from TRACE's 100-request heterogeneous account null. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05725v1)
- D03, Juuti et al., *PRADA*, EuroS&P 2019, inspected arXiv v3 November 16 2018. Full text verified. Tracks same-predicted-class query distances and representative samples; detects distributional irregularity. Authors explicitly concede sufficient Sybil clients circumvent it. Normality assumptions and image results require a new calibration for semantic text embeddings. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/1805.02628v3)
- D04, Zhang, Chen and Wagner, *SEAT: Similarity Encoder by Adversarial Training for Detecting Model Extraction Attack Queries*, AISec 2021, DOI 10.1145/3474369.3486863. Full author PDF verified. Adversarially trains similarity features and tests query blinding/filtering. Reports 3.8 to 16 times increased extraction cost, with a calibrated 0.01% benign-query FPR in its image setting. Neither figure establishes prevention, account-horizon false-positive control or detection of same-distribution distillation. [Source](https://surrealyz.github.io/files/pubs/seat-aisec2021.pdf)
- D05, Schwarzer et al., *AI Model Extraction Attacks: Bypassing Single-Client Assumptions in Defenses*, June 2 2026 arXiv v1, authors state May 2026 ICMCIS presentation. Full text verified. A 2,500-query MNIST attack split over 400 identities gives about six queries each, below a 50-query warm-up, making recall zero. Their global variant yields 83.1% recall; 99%-benign mixing yields 1% precision with 99.3% recall. The latter depends on attribution procedure, and is not a theorem about all global detectors. The cold-start failure is direct. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.03381v1)
- D06, Li et al., *Protecting Object Detection Models from Model Extraction Attack via Feature Space Coverage*, IJCAI 2024. Full PDF verified. Feature coverage nominates suspicious clients; active verification filters candidates. Coverage plus verification already exists as prior art. This is object detection, without LLM specialist or downstream transfer validation. [Source](https://www.ijcai.org/proceedings/2024/0048.pdf)
- D07, Li et al., *Extracting Robust Models with Uncertain Examples*, ICLR 2023 author PDF. Full text verified. BEST synthesizes uncertainty-based queries and evaluates defenses including PRADA and SEAT. Counterevidence to treating similarity detection as a finished solution. Its image/robustness objectives prevent numerical extrapolation to text. [Source](https://tianweiz07.github.io/Papers/23-iclr-1.pdf)
- D08, Kariyappa and Qureshi, *Defending Against Model Stealing Attacks with Adaptive Misinformation*, CVPR 2020 work, inspected arXiv PDF marked preprint. Full text verified. Corrupts OOD predictions, reports up to 40% clone-accuracy reduction with less than 0.5% benign-accuracy impact. Its essential premise is OOD queries; ID extraction removes that premise. Conditional-intervention antecedent, not justification for poisoning production answers indiscriminately. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.07100)
- D09, Krishna et al., *Thieves on Sesame Street! Model Extraction of BERT-based APIs*, ICLR 2020, arXiv v3 October 12 2020. Full PDF verified. Extracts using random-word and Wikipedia queries, costing hundreds of dollars under then-current prices. Tests sophisticated bypasses of membership/watermark defenses. A pretrained attacker starts with substantial capability. Specialized BERT costs/sample complexities do not describe frontier generative models. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.12366)
- D10, Zanella-Béguelin et al., *Grey-box Extraction of Natural Language Models*, ICML 2021. Full proceedings PDF verified. With a known frozen encoder and private linear head, in-distribution queries suffice for high-fidelity extraction. Concrete example of benign-looking acquisition; not evidence that a full proprietary transformer is recoverable with a few ID queries. [Source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/zanella-beguelin21a/zanella-beguelin21a.pdf)
- D11, Gudibande et al., *The False Promise of Imitating Proprietary LLMs*, May 25 2023 arXiv v1. Full PDF verified. Uses 1.5B to 13B bases and 0.3M to 150M imitation tokens. Style/human preference can improve without broad factual gains on tasks poorly covered by imitation data. A metric/base-model caution, not a claim that 2026 reasoning distillation fails. [Source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.15717)
- D12, Jiang, *DistillGuard: Evaluating Defenses Against LLM Knowledge Distillation*, March 8 2026 arXiv v1. Full text verified. Qwen3-14B to Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, 10,000 training samples, naive unfiltered attacker. CoT removal changes math score from 67.8% to 31.4%; coding is unaffected. Perturbation/poisoning often leave capabilities. Single-family and naive-attacker limits preclude a universal output-filter recommendation. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.07835v1)
- D13, *Stealing and Defending the Ends of LLMs*, ICLR 2026 OpenReview submission. Primary indexed PDF excerpt only, full retrieval blocked by browser challenge. Coverage-aware perturbation protects soft prompts. MMD paper attributes it to Kulkarni, Boenisch and Dziedzic, but inspected excerpt is anonymous. Do not assert accepted status or quantitative performance without an accessible original. Important coverage-driven-intervention prior art with narrower target. [Source](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=dOS3e1kC5u)

### Inferences

Proposed engineering, not a measured frontier-provider design:

1. Separate distribution anomaly, acquisition volume, campaign evidence and output-policy violations. A different corpus is not proof of extraction. Hard entitlements can limit acquisition without claiming intent. Challenges/review can precede denial.
2. Start with current embeddings and cheap independent feature families: exact/template hashes, input length, domains, mixture entropy, distinct prompt counts, repeat clusters, neighborhood novelty, session transitions, cumulative semantic coverage and released output tokens. PRADA/SEAT/coverage already cover much of this prior art. Correlated features do not count as independent corroboration. Explicit negative clients must include specialists, QA, batch labeling, translation, research and iterative coding.
3. Conditional MMD must match the normal unit. A specialist account window needs a domain/source-conditioned account reference, not a global mixed reference. Score domain mixture separately, since conditioning removes broad-coverage differences by design. Benign topic-ordered sessions belong in the null. Abstain for sparse strata.
4. Preserve cumulative/rolling distinct coverage to resist order-only evasion. Honest broad generalists look similar, so use coverage as budget weight or review evidence. Multiple window sizes add power for some shifts, memory and multiplicity burden, but cannot overcome identical observable distributions.
5. Campaign graphs should use verified organization/payment, specific infrastructure matches and rare-template/timing correlations. Office NAT, VPNs and public dataset prompts are weak edges. Avoid ban propagation by transitive closure of weak links. Store edge evidence, expire uncertain links and evaluate false merges/splits. Similarity retention introduces privacy cost.
6. Use bounded sketches/prototypes and reserve exact nearest-neighbor histories for candidates. A float32 384-dimensional embedding costs 1,536 bytes before indexing; 100 embeddings cost 153.6 kB/account; one million embeddings about 1.54 GB. Dense MMD is quadratic in batch size. Random features/cached kernels need power/numerical checks. Quotas/counters are O(1); streaming token ledgers need reservation/reconciliation to bound concurrent overshoot.
7. Count successful supervision released, not attempts. Labels, verified solutions, executable code, judgments and reasoning traces differ in training value. Tokens are auditable but incomplete. Dedup discounts repeated outputs, yet paraphrases evade naive dedup and low-entropy labels can be valuable. Choose raw and novelty-weighted budgets using task learning curves.
8. Mathematical identifiability: the rejection-probability difference of any detector observing transcript distributions P_a and P_b is bounded by total variation between those distributions. If they coincide, TPR = FPR. This follows directly from total variation's definition; it is not a new theorem. Metadata expands observables but separation cannot be assumed. With cheap unlinkable identities, per-account entitlements scale with identities. Friction raises acquisition cost without guaranteeing prevention.

### Gaps

No reviewed source establishes a deployment-validated LLM campaign graph with specialist false-block bounds, robust Sybil attribution, latency and defended-student capability under adaptive attacks together. Coverage, intent and linkage classifiers remain proposals until tested. No universal ordinary-answer distillation prevention method was established. Previously inspected local test accounts/attacks cannot be relabeled untouched.

## How should sequential evidence and calibration be rebuilt?

### Takeaway

Defensible conditional nulls precede accumulator choice. E-processes and CUSUM are established and already implemented. The research issue is a useful false-intervention guarantee under evolving client mixtures, shared sources, graph selection and drift.

### Cited Findings

- D14, Gretton et al., *A Kernel Two-Sample Test*, JMLR 13, March 2012. Full 51-page PDF verified. Gives MMD two-sample testing, unbiased quadratic/linear-time estimators and theory under sampling assumptions. Characteristic kernels separate distributions in population, but a lossy sentence encoder may collapse distinct raw-text distributions. MMD is not an intent classifier or an automatic finite-sample guarantee for a reused empirical null. [Source](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume13/gretton12a/gretton12a.pdf)
- D15, Liu et al., *Learning Deep Kernels for Non-Parametric Two-Sample Tests*, ICML 2020. Full proceedings PDF verified. Learns kernels to increase power with split training/testing. Better justified than blindly swapping encoders. Not extraction detection, and learned separation may memorize corpus/generator identity. [Source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/liu20m/liu20m.pdf)
- D16, Lopez-Paz and Oquab, *Revisiting Classifier Two-Sample Tests*, ICLR 2017, arXiv v3 checked. Full text verified. A binary classifier trained on one split tests separation on another. Useful detectability diagnostic alongside MMD, but source-disjoint account-level splits are necessary to avoid mistaking corpus identity for extraction. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/1610.06545v3)
- D17, Howard et al., *Time-uniform Chernoff bounds via nonnegative supermartingales*, Probability Surveys 2020, arXiv v8 December 17 2025. Full text verified. Time-uniform crossing bounds require stated martingale assumptions. A product of arbitrary empirical scores is not automatically anytime valid. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/1808.03204v8)
- D18, Shin, Ramdas and Rinaldo, *E-detectors: a nonparametric framework for sequential change detection*, arXiv v4 October 29 2023 checked. Full text verified. Sums processes begun at successive candidate changepoints; CUSUM/Shiryaev-Roberts-style procedures control average run length. ARL differs from probability of ever crossing. Resetting/flooring a process does not inherit an unreset process's Ville guarantee. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.03532v4)
- D19, Barber, Candès, Ramdas and Tibshirani, *Conformal Prediction Beyond Exchangeability*, Annals of Statistics 2023, arXiv v3 checked. Full text verified. Quantifies drift/nonexchangeability effects under assumptions. It does not make arbitrarily pooled account ranks conditionally valid; the exchangeable unit must be specified. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.13415v3)
- D20, Bhattacharyya and Ramdas, *Change detection with conformal martingales: new optimal constructions, and suboptimality of existing methods*, September 23 2026 arXiv v1. Full text accessible, relevant introduction checked, proofs not audited. Under independent observations, older constructions can incur poor delays; new constructions improve theoretical orders. Recent reset/adaptation prior art, not a guaranteed delay for correlated LLM streams. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27179v1)
- D21, *Non-partitioned e-detectors for nonparametric sequential change detection*, July 30 2026 arXiv v1. Full text accessible, abstract/method outline checked, proofs not audited. Aggregates point-null processes across changepoints and infimizes over candidate nulls. Relevant composite-benign prior art; practical cost/assumptions still need study. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.28322v1)

### Inferences

Near term, use explicitly empirical account-horizon thresholds. Fit references, score nulls and policy horizons in separate roles. Calibrate specialists, repetitive interactive clients, batch users and generalists, separated by source family and time. Report worst-group false intervention and intervals. Select feature maps, detector direction, window sizes and fusion on development sources, freeze, then evaluate newly reserved source families and a temporal drift period.

For a formal arm, define the filtration. Conditional superuniform p_t gives e_t = eta * p_t^(eta-1), with 0 < eta < 1, conditional expectation at most one. The product supports Ville thresholds. TRACE's fixed overlapping-window null does not establish those conditional premises. Nonoverlapping windows remove only one dependence; shared sources and latent account topics remain.

A fixed convex mixture of valid e-processes retains validity under dependence. Multiplying sensor processes needs appropriate conditional conditions; maximizing sensors requires error allocation. Predictable adaptation or independent splits are necessary for learned score changes. Choosing clusters after seeing anomalous observations is selection, so naive cluster p-values are invalid. Keep graph decisions empirically evaluated unless selective validity is proved. At deployment scale even 1% account-horizon false alarms can overwhelm reviewers; alert and hard-denial targets must differ.

E-CUSUM/restarts improve late-onset responsiveness when plain products have banked negative evidence, but require their own ARL/repeated-testing calibration. Do not label a reset process “ever false block <= alpha” without the appropriate proof. The repo already guards Ville thresholds. New contribution must address dependency, conditional calibration and grouping, not rename established sequential methods.

### Gaps

No verified guarantee covers unknown source mixtures, adaptive graph merges, user-selected tiers, drift and intervention censoring together here. Without a full model, use empirical claims. Asymptotic delay results are not deployment promises.

## What should be built and measured, and what could be original?

### Takeaway

A promising capstone is capability acquisition under specialist-safe, identity-aware budgets, with conditional detection as an auxiliary sensor. MMD plus PRADA plus quotas is an implementation built on prior art. Research originality needs a new objective, construction, protocol or empirical finding against coverage and lifecycle baselines.

### Cited Findings

- D22, Douceur, *The Sybil Attack*, IPTPS 2002. Microsoft primary publication page checked, full paper not audited. Identity-multiplicity result under distributed-system assumptions motivates scarce/verified entitlements. Not a theorem about the economics of a paid AI API. [Source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-sybil-attack/)
- D23, Anthropic, *Detecting and preventing distillation attacks*, February 23 2026. Official full text checked. Reports 16 million-plus exchanges through roughly 24,000 fraudulent accounts; classifiers/behavioral fingerprints plus metadata, infrastructure, payment and timing correlations. Provider claims without public ROC, control population, ablation or student-capability-reduction measure. Evidence for layered practice, not a reproducible accuracy target. Latest October provider material is covered by the other researcher. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/detecting-and-preventing-distillation-attacks)
- D24, Hasan, *Bounded Behavioral Indistinguishability for Black-Box LLM Distillation*, May 28 2026 arXiv v1. Abstract only checked. Distinguishes semantic similarity from bounded teacher/student identification and reports disagreement-guided acquisition not consistently outperforming stratified random sampling. A metric-design lead, not verified deployment evidence. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30448)

Contribution candidate A: capability-normalized acquisition under uncertain identities. Measure task learning curves G_c(B), optimize entitlements/challenges against student gain and workflow loss, compare raw requests/tokens with novel supervision-weighted budgets. Attackers optimize useful supervision per entitlement. Closest prior art is D06 coverage, D13 coverage-aware perturbation and D01 lifecycle evaluation. Originality might reside in joint control/learning-curve objective, not coverage or ensembles. Broader novelty search required.

Further closest-prior-art check for acquisition efficiency: [D25, Chandrasekaran et al., USENIX Security 2020](https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity20/presentation/chandrasekaran), formalizes extraction and draws active-learning connections. [D26, Orekondy et al., Knockoff Nets, CVPR 2019](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Orekondy_Knockoff_Nets_Stealing_Functionality_of_Black-Box_Models_CVPR_2019_paper.html), already adapts query selection for sample efficiency. [D27, Pal et al., ActiveThief, AAAI 2020](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/5432), reports public-data active learning and PRADA evasion. These three were checked at the official abstract level only. The last reports querying 10-30% of a public dataset, which is not a fraction of the teacher's training set or a universal query budget. Thus “optimize useful examples per query” is established attacker prior art. Novelty would need the defender's specialist-utility-constrained policy under uncertain campaign linkage and a tested transfer metric. This scoped search did not establish that this exact formulation is new.

Candidate B: specialist-safe composite nulls with uncertain campaign attribution. Compare global, account, verified-organization and inferred-cluster detectors at fixed attacker spend. Report worst-group intervention and false merges. D02-D05 and D18 already cover windows, geometry, Sybil failure and accumulation. A specific construction/guarantee or demonstrated utility-attribution frontier must be new.

Candidate C: online adaptive same-source acquisition benchmark. Reserve entire sources/workflows and cross reordering, paraphrase, filler, slow pacing, 1/5/20/100 identities, churn, rewriting and student-side filtering. Compare student gain to base-student and alternate/public-teacher controls. D01 already has lifecycle claims. Differentiation would be actual enforcement, identity ambiguity, real utility and cost-constrained adaptive acquisition, rather than another static attack/defense table.

Suggested preregistered acceptance criteria:

- Report account-horizon false denial and completed-workflow utility separately by client class. An illustrative target is <=1% hard denial with one-sided 95% bound below target. Even with independent accounts, zero failures needs about 299 accounts per group; shared sources reduce effective evidence. Graph false merges and appeals are separate endpoints.
- Measure student capability reduction at matched legitimate utility. Plot scores versus released responses, output tokens, wall time and attacker spend per task. Include budget-only controls so detection is credited only for incremental benefit.
- Define deadlines. Rate limits can slow collection without changing eventual capability; benchmark honest batch use before selecting limits.
- Report actual restrictions, release before first restriction, per-task supervision, graph precision/recall and false merges, latency p50/p95, CPU cost and retained bytes. Bootstrap by source/campaign; describe a few campaigns as cases rather than fake independent windows.
- Shadow first, develop on development data, freeze model/config/calibration hashes, evaluate once on untouched sources then separately under drift. Never let inspected attacks or appeals silently enter the calibration role.
- CPU-only replay can develop budgets/graphs/features. Isolated embedding profiling and separately scheduled student training establish costs; no hardware runtime was measured here. Existing audit caches verify engineering but cannot recreate untouched confirmation data.

Incremental architecture can retain authentication, canonical logging, replay and action APIs. Separate released-supervision ledger, conditional score service, campaign evidence store and action policy. First enforce transparent account/organization entitlements through current action machinery. Run detectors in shadow mode until they beat budget-only controls at matched honest utility. This is a substantial rebuild of statistical/decision modules without deleting verified infrastructure.

### Gaps

No novelty guarantee. CAP full text retrieval is blocked and the very recent lifecycle preprint is not independently reproduced. Other researchers cover watermarking, reasoning protection and provider disclosures. “Prevents distillation” must be bounded by signals, identity cost, timescale, provider access and target capability. Preventing all training on ordinary answers while preserving ordinary access has not been shown feasible.
